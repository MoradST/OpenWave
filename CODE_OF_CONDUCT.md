# OpenWave — Community Code of Conduct

**Our goal:** a welcoming, useful, open space where people, builders, communities and brands can connect through shared posts and AI-assisted discovery.

## Be welcome

- Be respectful and curious. Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Credit original creators, disclose relevant affiliations, and distinguish facts from rumors.
- Welcome newcomers and help them discover the right channel, including Members and Brands.
- Share projects, events and opportunities where they fit; avoid repetitive or unsolicited promotion.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, hate, threats, stalking, sexual harassment or discriminatory abuse.
- Doxxing, exposing other people's private data, location tracking or revealing confidential material.
- Impersonating a person or organization, fake endorsements, scams, malware, phishing or deceptive links.
- Spam, automated flooding, coordinated manipulation or intentionally misleading posts.
- Dangerous instructions or unverified emergency claims presented as authoritative safety guidance.

## Public profiles and AI assistance

Member and Brand profiles are self-declared, not identity-verified. Using an AI assistant does not remove accountability for what is posted through your GitHub account. Do not prompt an assistant to publish another person's private information.

## Enforcement and reports

Posts do not require pre-approval. Repository maintainers may remove or restrict abusive material when they become aware of it; GitHub may also enforce platform policies. There is no promise of active 24/7 or comprehensive automated moderation.

For removal requests or problematic content, please use any contact information listed on [@MoradST's GitHub profile](https://github.com/MoradST). For non-sensitive reports, you can also [open a public Issue](https://github.com/MoradST/OpenWave/issues/new) with a link to the relevant content. GitHub has no regular private DMs. **Do not share sensitive information in public Issues.** For abuse or privacy-sensitive complaints, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) when appropriate. OpenWave does not currently provide a dedicated private support inbox.

## Other policies

Read the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md), [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md), and [GitHub Acceptable Use Policies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies).

This Code of Conduct is a community policy for the OpenWave experiment. It may evolve as the network grows.
